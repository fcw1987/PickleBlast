# Changelog

## Unreleased

The current development candidate is version 1.0 (6), awaiting independent user testing and acceptance. No public release history is listed here.

- Added a night-arena presentation with an opaque black court, clearer line hierarchy, fine net detail, subdued background, compact HUD framing and character foot markers. Original character frames and contact timing remain intact.
- Boss Rally appears first on Home, with Arcade below it. The opponent menu lists all five bosses individually.
- After a win, Play Next offers Wall → Banger → Poacher → Dinker → Lobber in displayed order. The Lobber ends the list without looping; Rematch, Choose Opponent, and Home stay available.
- Added The Dinker and The Lobber as individual Boss Rally opponents, with approved motion artwork and separate local records.
- The Dinker mixes short soft resets into ordinary exchanges. The Lobber uses a committed elevated arc that descends into the existing automatic return area.
- Banger drives have a distinct pace within the existing speed cap and response-time limit. Poacher cues now follow its committed movement target.
- Matches remain first to three with two saves. Play Next begins a fresh individual match. Wall behavior, receiving help, and controls are preserved.
- Arcade includes three target waves and a final rally against The Wall.
- Crown and court drag control player movement; returns happen automatically.
- Settings and local Arcade and boss records are saved on the Watch.
