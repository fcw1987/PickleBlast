# Changelog

## Unreleased

Version 1.0 (10) was physically tested and accepted by the owner on October 5, 2026. Earlier builds remain preserved in git history. No public release history is listed here.

- Added the selected B3 blue acrylic playing surface, darker regulation kitchen, painted white lines and a layered mesh net, with separately authored illustrated skyline and palms. Compact black HUD framing and character grounding remain. Original character frames and contact timing are intact.
- Boss Rally appears first on Home, with Arcade below it. The opponent menu lists all five bosses individually.
- After a win, Play Next offers Wall → Banger → Poacher → Dinker → Lobber in displayed order. The Lobber ends the list without looping; Rematch, Choose Opponent, and Home stay available.
- Added The Dinker and The Lobber as individual Boss Rally opponents, with approved motion artwork and separate local records.
- The Dinker mixes short soft resets into ordinary exchanges. The Lobber uses a committed elevated arc that descends into the existing automatic return area.
- Banger drives have a distinct pace within the existing speed cap and response-time limit. Poacher cues now follow its committed movement target.
- Boss Rally remains first to three, with no starting save. Every 20 consecutive player returns can earn a save, capped at one. Won points preserve return progress and an unused save. Player misses, including a consumed save or lost point, reset progress; rematch, restart and Play Next start fresh. Milestones replace the raw on-screen rally counter. Play Next begins a fresh individual match. Wall behavior, receiving help, and controls are preserved.
- Arcade includes three target waves and a final rally against The Wall, with original paddle/cone/basket sprites and bounded hit recoil using existing pooled effects.
- The approved matte chartreuse icon combines a flowing true-black paddle outline and five pickleball holes. The moon stays clear of the native clock.
- Crown and court drag control player movement; returns happen automatically.
- Settings and local Arcade and boss records are saved on the Watch.
