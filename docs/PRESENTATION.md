# Watch presentation

The rendering-only projection preserves logical regulation geometry. The near baseline is 1.5625 times the far baseline width. Safe-area insets place the HUD beneath the clock, the far baseline below the HUD and the near baseline near the lower usable edge. Background aspect fill is decorative; court lines and sprites are independently projected. Outside remains true black.

Approved complete-frame characters are upright square billboards sized from fixed alpha unions. Current player alpha-union height is about 47.04 points at a 211-point viewport, scaling for narrower displays. Whole-frame translation aligns the embedded paddle with authoritative ball contact without redesigning animation or enlarging collision reach. The ball renders above the player on centered blocks. Target paddle heads sit at their projected colliders; handles do not define hits.

Pause has a 44 × 44 point target in the safe header. The native render host cannot intercept touches. Crown/drag input excludes that header and stops while paused. Resume and Home are readily available; Restart follows below.

Original timestamps, alpha, grip continuity and background are preserved. Effects retain logical origins and reproject on layout changes without advancing paused lifetime. Celebration uses bounded existing particles followed by blackout.

[README screenshots](../README.md) are genuine Watch simulator captures. [Testing](TESTING.md) distinguishes simulator automation from physical play. Simulator images do not certify Crown feel, haptics, battery or on-wrist readability.
