Quick answers

A few things to try.

How do I move and return the ball?

Turn the Digital Crown or drag across the court to move your player. Get beneath the incoming ball and the return happens automatically. Center contact sends the ball straight; edge contact aims left or right. You don’t need to swing your wrist.

The Crown feels too fast or too slow.

From Home, open Settings and adjust Crown sensitivity. You can also drag across the court during gameplay. Your sensitivity setting is saved locally between launches.

How do I pause, resume, or restart?

Tap Pause at the top of the game. Choose Resume to continue, Home to leave the match, or Restart to begin again. If an interruption pauses the game, choose Resume; a countdown gives you time to get ready.

Leaving a match does not erase your saved settings or records. A match in progress is not restored if the app process ends.

How do saves and scoring work?

Boss Rally is first to three points. Each match has two free saves; those saves do not award a point and do not refill between points. After they’re used, a player miss awards the boss a point. A boss miss awards you a point.

All Three plays The Wall, The Banger, then The Poacher. A win starts the next match with a fresh score and two saves. Arcade gives three lives across the run and two free recoveries per stage.

How do I change haptics?

Open Settings from Home and use the Haptics switch. Feedback occurs during gameplay events and pauses when the game is inactive. The preference is saved on your Watch.

What information helps with a bug report?

Include the app version and build, Watch model, watchOS version, game mode, steps to reproduce, what you expected, and what actually happened. Open the game’s Settings and look for Version to find the app version and build.

A screenshot is optional. Remove personal information before attaching it. Please don’t include your Watch serial number, device identifier, Apple account details, or complete device logs.

Does PickleBlast need an iPhone or internet connection?

PickleBlast is a standalone Apple Watch game. Gameplay works offline and has no in-app account or iPhone companion. The game targets watchOS 10 or later; that target does not establish testing on every model or OS version.
